macOS has two ways into sleep and they do not share a switch. Every keep-awake app you have tried holds the wrong one.

The lid never reads an assertion

An idle assertion tells your Mac nobody has touched it. A lid close is a sensor, and it does not ask. StayUp raises the one flag that overrides it, and takes it back down when the work is done.

  • Holds through a closed lid, on battery and on the cable
  • Never left up by accident, three ways
  • No kernel extension, no login shim, no daemon watching you type

On 3 September something set that flag and never put it back. For fifty-eight hours this Mac did not sleep once, lid open or lid shut, and nothing in the interface said so. StayUp is the same flag with a deadline on it.

Questions worth asking

It asks for root once. You should want to know what it does with it.

Four things. A one-line rule in /etc/sudoers.d/stayup letting your user run exactly two commands without a password. A boot job that clears the flag at every start. A user job that runs every 60 seconds and clears a flag nobody is claiming. And a lease file it rewrites while a session runs.
Settings, Helper, Remove. It boots out both jobs, deletes both files and the rule, and puts the flag down on its way past. The README lists the five commands if you would rather do it by hand.
Because a lid close does not consult it. Measured here: an idle assertion was live at 23:07:51 and the log read Entering Sleep state due to 'Clamshell Sleep' eleven seconds later. The assertion held the whole time and the Mac slept anyway.
No, and it never asks. The shortcut is registered with the system rather than read off your keyboard, so StayUp only ever hears the one combination it claimed.
The lease stops being renewed and goes stale in two minutes. Inside another minute the guard finds it, clears the flag and writes down why. Force-quit, hang and crash are each covered by that; a restart is covered by the boot job.
It stops at 15% and comes back on the cable or at 20%. Turn on "only while charging" and it never raises the flag off the cable at all.
